03 — Culture

Small Team.
Large Mandate.

We believe the best work happens when exceptional people are trusted completely. Our culture is built around ownership, candour, and the conviction that finance and technology are better when they work as one.

"We don't hire for roles. We hire for calibre — and then build the role around the person."
🎯
Radical Ownership
Every team member owns their domain end-to-end. We expect you to make decisions, flag risks, and drive outcomes without waiting for permission.
🔬
Rigour Without Bureaucracy
High standards, low overhead. We invest heavily in quality thinking but eliminate the meetings, decks, and processes that don't move the needle.
🌏
Asia-First Perspective
Grounded in Singapore and Taiwan, we approach global markets with an Asian capital markets lens — nuanced, networked, and ahead of the curve.
⚡
Always Learning
We allocate budget and time for continuous learning — conferences, courses, research subscriptions. Intellectual growth is not optional; it's core to the role.
